# BrightPath Solutions – Work From Home FAQ

**How many days can I work from home?**
Employees can work from home up to 2 days per week. Tuesday and Thursday are office days for everyone, so team meetings can happen in person.

**Do I need approval?**
Yes. Work-from-home days must be marked in the HR portal by the end of the previous working day. Your manager can ask you to come to the office on a work-from-home day if there is an urgent need.

**What equipment does the company provide?**
The company provides a laptop, a headset and a one-time home office allowance of ₹8,000 for a chair or desk. The allowance must be claimed within 6 months of joining.

**Is internet paid for?**
Employees get an internet reimbursement of ₹1,000 per month. Submit the bill through the HR portal by the 5th of the next month.

**What are the working hours?**
Core working hours are 10 AM to 4 PM, when everyone must be available online. Outside core hours, employees can choose their own schedule as long as they work 8 hours a day.

**Can I work from another city?**
Working from another city within India is allowed for up to 2 weeks per year with manager approval. Working from outside India is not allowed.
