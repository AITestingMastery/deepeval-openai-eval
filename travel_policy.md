# BrightPath Solutions – Business Travel Policy

## Approval
All business travel must be approved by the reporting manager before booking. International travel also needs approval from the department head.

## Flights and trains
Employees travel in economy class for all domestic flights. For international flights longer than 8 hours, managers and above may travel in premium economy. Train travel is allowed in AC 2-tier or AC 3-tier.

## Hotels
The hotel limit is ₹5,000 per night in metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata) and ₹3,500 per night in other cities. Bookings should be made through the company travel desk.

## Daily allowance
Employees receive a daily allowance of ₹1,200 for food and local travel in metro cities, and ₹900 in other cities. No bills are needed for the daily allowance.

## Local transport
Cab rides for business purposes are reimbursed with receipts. Personal vehicle use is reimbursed at ₹10 per km for cars and ₹4 per km for two-wheelers.

## Claims
Expense claims must be submitted within 15 days of returning from the trip, with original bills. Claims submitted after 30 days will not be reimbursed. Approved claims are paid with the next month's salary.
