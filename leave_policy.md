# BrightPath Solutions – Employee Leave Policy

Effective from 1 April 2026. Applies to all full-time employees in India.

## Casual leave
Employees get 12 casual leaves per calendar year. Casual leave can be taken for personal work or short trips. A maximum of 3 casual leaves can be taken in a row. Unused casual leave cannot be carried forward to the next year and lapses on 31 December.

## Sick leave
Employees get 10 paid sick leaves per year. A medical certificate is required if sick leave is taken for more than 2 days in a row. Unused sick leave can be carried forward, up to a maximum of 30 days in total.

## Earned leave
Employees earn 1.5 days of earned leave for every month worked, which is 18 days per year. Earned leave must be applied for at least 7 days in advance. Up to 45 days of earned leave can be carried forward. At resignation, unused earned leave is paid out at the basic salary rate.

## Maternity and paternity leave
Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave for the first two children. Male employees get 10 working days of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 3 months of the child's birth.

## Public holidays
The company follows 10 fixed public holidays and 2 optional holidays each year. The holiday list is published by HR every December.

## How to apply
All leave must be applied for through the HR portal. Your reporting manager approves or rejects the request within 2 working days. For questions, email hr@brightpath.example.
